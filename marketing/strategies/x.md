# X (Twitter) Engagement Strategy for xray

Goal: Build audience, surface pain points, create viral moments, and launch with community momentum.

---

## Strategy: Pain Discovery → Audience Building → Launch Momentum

### Phase 1: Pain Discovery & Audience Building (Days 1-4)
Tweet pain points, start conversations, build followers who resonate.

### Phase 2: Building in Public (Days 5-7)
Share progress, tease solution, build anticipation.

### Phase 3: Launch & Viral Push (Days 8-10)
Product launch, demo videos, user testimonials, momentum.

---

## Key X/Twitter Dynamics

### What Works on X

✅ **Short, punchy tweets** (280 chars or thread)
✅ **Personal stories** with vulnerability
✅ **Data/numbers** (people retweet stats)
✅ **Hot takes** (controversial but defensible)
✅ **Memes & humor** (technical memes perform well)
✅ **Build-in-public** transparency
✅ **Quote tweets** with added insight
✅ **Threads** with clear structure
✅ **Polls** for engagement
✅ **Screenshots** of metrics/results

### What Doesn't Work

❌ **Long paragraphs** (use threads instead)
❌ **Corporate speak** (be human)
❌ **Link-only tweets** (context first, link in thread)
❌ **Too many hashtags** (1-2 max)
❌ **Begging for RTs** (earn them with value)
❌ **Overly polished** (raw > perfect on X)

## Content Strategy: The 5 Tweet Types

### 1. Pain Point Tweets (Days 1-2)
**Goal**: Surface pain, get engagement, build audience

**Format**: Personal story + question

**Example**:
```
I spend 40% of my "coding time" explaining my codebase to AI.

Is this normal or am I using AI tools wrong?

The overhead:
• 30-45 min finding relevant files
• Copying code
• AI says "I don't see X"
• Copy more
• Hit token limit
• Start over

Should be faster, feels slower.
```

**Why it works**:
- Relatable pain
- Specific numbers
- Ends with question (invites replies)
- Personal vulnerability

---

### 2. Data/Insight Tweets (Days 3-4)
**Goal**: Show you've researched, provide value

**Format**: Surprising data + insight

**Example**:
```
I tracked my AI coding time for 2 weeks.

Results surprised me:

Actual coding: 42%
AI context prep: 28%
Fixing AI mistakes: 18%
Testing: 12%

That 28% preparing context = 11 hours per week.

I thought AI would save time.
It does - but there's a hidden tax nobody talks about.
```

**Why it works**:
- Numbers get attention
- Structured (easy to scan)
- Surprising insight
- People quote-tweet with their numbers

## Growth Tactics

### 1. Engage Before You Post

**Days -7 to 0 (before campaign)**:
- Reply to 20-30 tweets daily in your niche
- Add value, don't promote
- Build recognition
- Gain 50-100 followers who'll see your posts

### 2. Thread Strategy

**Why threads work**:
- More real estate for your message
- Twitter promotes threads
- People read to the end
- Easy to bookmark and share

**Thread structure**:
1. Hook (surprising stat or question)
2. Context (why this matters)
3. Insights (3-5 key points)
4. Conclusion (call to action)

### 3. Quote Tweet Strategy

**Find tweets about**:
- AI coding pain points
- Developer productivity
- Onboarding struggles

**Quote tweet with**:
- Your experience

### 4. Collaborate

**Partner with**:
- Other build-in-public founders

**Cross-promote**:
- Guest threads

- Mutual testimonials

**What performs well**:

- Code snippets (syntax highlighted)
- Simple diagrams
- Memes
- Demo GIFs

**Tools**:
